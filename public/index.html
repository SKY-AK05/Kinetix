How Kinetix Processes Authorized Media

A transparent 4-stage pipeline engineered for source fidelity, security, and zero upscaling deception.

STEP 01 • URL & SSRF VALIDATION

Strict Domain Allowlist & Network Guard

Every submitted URL is parsed and validated against approved Creative Commons and open-media hosts. Requests targeting localhost, private RFC 1918 IPs (127.0.0.1, 10.x, 192.168.x), or cloud metadata endpoints (169.254.169.254) are rejected at the edge.

STEP 02 • REAL STREAM PROBING

Authentic 10-Tier Resolution Ladder

The backend inspects the source manifest via ffprobe and unlocks only resolutions genuinely present in the source master (from 144p up to 8K 4320p). Unavailable tiers are explicitly disabled rather than faked or upscaled.

STEP 03 • FFMPEG WORKER MUXING

Separate Video/Audio Muxing & MP3 Extraction

High-resolution streams (1080p through 8K) deliver separate adaptive video and audio tracks. Our background worker combines them into a fast-start H.264/AAC MP4 container or transcodes audio to 128/192/320 kbps MP3 with ID3v2 tags.

STEP 04 • SIGNED EPHEMERAL DELIVERY

HMAC-SHA256 Tokens & Automatic Cleanup

Completed files are verified for non-zero integrity and served via session-bound, short-lived signed URLs (15-minute TTL). Expired and cancelled jobs are automatically purged from temporary storage.

System Architecture & Responsible Use

Built with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Zod, BullMQ/Redis, and FFmpeg worker isolation.

Install Kinetix directly from Google Chrome to launch in a standalone window from your Android home screen or desktop dock.
